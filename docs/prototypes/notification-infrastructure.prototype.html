What this comparison can and cannot tell you

The common application work does not disappear: authorize recipients, preserve source activity and notification intent together, recover failures without duplicates, enforce current visibility, and preserve original expiry. A provider does not make an accepted comment depend on a live provider request.

Proposed ownership: first-party keeps inbox storage, delivery logic, and operations with the app maintainer. Self-hosted adds notification-service deployments and their backing stores. Managed outsources service operation but leaves integration, privacy approval, plan limits, and incident coordination with the app maintainer. Actual product fit still needs verification.

Not established here: real transaction durability, provider idempotency windows, cross-device read synchronization, enforceable access boundaries, 90-day deletion/export support, 60-second freshness, burst capacity, hosting requirements, or total cost. No option is scored or recommended by this simulator.

Human verdict: after trying the scenarios, record which ownership model you prefer, which assumptions are unacceptable, and what needs a real technical spike. The infrastructure decision remains open until you make it.
